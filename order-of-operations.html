Order of operations

When an expression mixes signs, the order matters. Multiplication happens before addition and subtraction. Brackets come first of all. Then you work left to right.

Why it matters

Take 2 + 3 × 4. If you add first you get 20. If you multiply first you get 2 + 12 = 14. The agreed answer is 14.

Steps

  1. Do anything inside brackets.
  2. Do multiplication.
  3. Do addition and subtraction from left to right.
Example. (14 − 6) × 2 + 14. Brackets: 8. Then 8 × 2 = 16. Then 16 + 14 = 30.

Common slip

With 10 − 4 + 3, go left to right: 6 + 3 = 9. Do not do 4 + 3 first, that gives 3, which is wrong.
