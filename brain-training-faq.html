Does brain training work? An honest FAQ

Will puzzles make me smarter?

Practice makes you better at the kind of puzzle you practice. Research on whether brain-training games raise general intelligence or everyday thinking is mixed, and we do not claim that Brainloop does. Think of it like a crossword: a satisfying five-minute habit and a way to learn tricks.

Is Brainloop an IQ test?

No. A real IQ test is given and scored under standard conditions by a trained professional. Brainloop is a casual game. It does not give an IQ score and you should not use it to judge your intelligence.

Where do the puzzles come from?

A program generates them from simple rules and a seed based on the date. That means every set is original, and answers are checked by code before you see them. Each daily set is the same for everyone that day.

What do you collect about me?

Nothing is sent anywhere. Your streak, stats and progress are stored on your device only. Read the privacy page.

Can I play offline?

Yes, after the first visit. Add Brainloop to your home screen and it works without a connection.

How do I get better?

  • Play the daily set at the same time each day.
  • Read the short guides: sequences, logic, mini sudoku, mental math.
  • After a wrong answer, read the explanation instead of skipping it.
