Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: Brainloop does not collect, send or sell any personal information.

What is stored on your device

To remember your streak, your stats and your progress in today's set, Brainloop uses your browser's local storage on your own device. It also stores the app files so the game can work offline. This data never leaves your device, and you can clear it any time by clearing site data in your browser.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No cookies set by Brainloop.
  • No data sent to servers that we run. Brainloop has no server.

Children

Brainloop is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to collect from children either.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. GitHub, like any host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's own privacy statement for how it handles them.

Changes

If we ever add advertising or any other service that handles data, this page will be updated first to explain exactly what changes, and the game will ask where the law requires it.
