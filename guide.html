How to play Brainloop

Brainloop gives you one set of five short puzzles each day. Everyone gets the same set on the same date, so you can compare your result with friends. A set takes about five minutes.

The five puzzles

  • Number sequence. Find the rule and pick the next number. Read the sequence guide.
  • Logic. A few clues about who is taller or shorter. Put them in order. Read the logic guide.
  • Odd one out. Five numbers, one breaks the pattern.
  • Mental math. A short sum with the right order of operations. Read the math guide.
  • Mini sudoku. A 4 by 4 grid using 1 to 4. Read the sudoku guide.

Scoring

Each multiple-choice puzzle gives you one point if you pick the right answer. You get one try. For the sudoku, a clean solve with no wrong checks gives one point, and a solve after one or more wrong checks gives half a point. Your daily score is out of five.

Streaks

Finish a daily set and your streak goes up by one. Miss a whole day and it resets to one the next time you finish. Your streak and stats are stored only on your own device.

Practice mode

When you want more, use Practice on the home screen. Pick one puzzle type or mix them. Practice puzzles are random and do not change your streak.

Sharing

After a daily set, tap Share result. You get a short block of colored squares: green for a first-try solve, yellow for a sudoku solved after a wrong check, black for a miss. It does not reveal answers.

Install it

Brainloop works in your browser. To add it to your phone, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app and keeps itself up to date.

A note on the name of this game: Brainloop is for fun and practice. It is not a clinical or standardized IQ test, and it does not measure your IQ.
