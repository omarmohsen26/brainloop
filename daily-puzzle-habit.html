How to build a daily puzzle habit

A good puzzle habit is small. Five minutes a day is easier to keep than an hour once a week, and that is why Brainloop gives you one short set each day.

Attach it to something you already do

Pair the puzzle with a cue: your morning coffee, your commute, or the minute before bed. A cue you already have is easier to remember than a reminder.

Keep the bar low

On a busy day, finish the set even if you rush. A fast attempt keeps the routine alive. A streak freeze covers one missed day, so a single bad day does not undo your progress.

Learn from misses

After a wrong answer, read the short explanation. Most puzzles repeat a small set of ideas, so a ten-second read makes you faster next time. The guides list them type by type.

Use hard mode when it feels easy

When you score five out of five for a week, try the hard set. It uses the same ideas with extra steps.

Compare, do not compete

Share your result card with a friend and see how you did on the same set. The colored squares do not give away the answers.
