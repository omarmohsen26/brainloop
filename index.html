What is Brainloop?

Brainloop is a free daily puzzle game made of five short puzzles: number sequences, logic questions, odd one out, mental math and a 4 by 4 mini sudoku. The set is new every day and the same for every player, so you can compare results with friends. Build a streak, practice any type you like, and read the guide to learn the tricks.

It is built to be quick and calm, with no timers, no accounts and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device, and it works offline once opened. It is a game for fun and practice, not an IQ test.
