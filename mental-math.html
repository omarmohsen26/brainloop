Mental math: simple methods that work

Order of operations

Do brackets first. Then multiplication and division. Then addition and subtraction. So 12 + 3 × 4 is 12 + 12 = 24, not 15 × 4 = 60. Brainloop puzzles always use this order, and the explanation after each puzzle shows the steps.

Multiplying by a teen number

Split it. 14 × 6 is 10 × 6 plus 4 × 6, which is 60 + 24 = 84. This split method works for any two-digit number.

Adding bigger numbers

Add the tens first, then the ones. 47 + 38: 40 + 30 = 70, then 7 + 8 = 15, then 70 + 15 = 85.

Divide by thinking "how many groups"

56 ÷ 7 asks how many sevens fit in 56. If you know your seven times table, the answer is 8. If a division is hard, check it by multiplying back.

Rule of thumb for checking

Round the numbers first and estimate. 19 × 4 is close to 20 × 4 = 80, so the real answer should be a little below 80 (it is 76). If one of the four choices is far from your estimate, drop it.

Try this: 8 + 6 × 5. Multiply first: 6 × 5 = 30. Then add: 38.
