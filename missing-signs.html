Missing signs: find the operators

You get an equation with the signs hidden, like 4 ? 6 ? 5 = 15. Your job is to choose the signs that make it true. In Brainloop, multiplication is done before addition and subtraction, just like in school math.

Method

  1. Look at the target. Is it much bigger than the numbers? Then a times sign is likely.
  2. Is it close to the first number? Then plus or minus with small numbers is likely.
  3. Try the most likely choice and work it out, multiplying first.
  4. If the result is wrong, change one sign and try again.
Example. 4 ? 6 ? 5 = 15. Try + and +: 4 + 6 + 5 = 15. It fits. Try × and +: 24 + 5 = 29, too big.

Use the answer choices

You only have four choices. Test each one quickly and cross it out. Often two of them are far off and can be dropped at once.

Hard mode

Hard mode has three signs. Do the multiplications first, then add and subtract from left to right. Write the partial result as you go.
